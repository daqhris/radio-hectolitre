About

Radio Hectolitre

Radio Hectolitre is an internet art radio broadcast by a resident at Hectolitre, an artists-run space in Brussels. It broadcasts live performances, artist interviews, concerts, electroacoustic music, and more sonic experiences happening in the space and publishes documentation and recordings afterwards.

Format

Each broadcast page combines a live stream link, a listen shelf of recordings, photo or film documentation, spoken words or liner notes of a performance, and a running audio library. External links embedding catalogue from online platforms are present and encoded to interweave web traces.

Infrastructure

Open Code Source (git repository)· Hosted on GitHub Pages · Creator, coder, maintainer: daqhris · Studio: Hectolitre, Marolles, Brussels · Online since August 5, 2026 (construction-in-progress)

Station

The core idea is a synced station without a backend server. There's not one continuous shared audio stream produced the way a typical internet-radio setup does. Tapping play seeks to the current computed position and starts playback. Every listener's browser independently computes "what should be playing right now" from a fixed program and the wall clock — landing on the same track and offset without any server or listener-to-listener coordination.

For podcast apps & RSS readers: feed.xml · Plain playlist file: station.m3u
