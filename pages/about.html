Radio Hectolitre

Radio Hectolitre is a project by daqhris, resident at Hectolitre, an artists-run space in Brussels. It broadcasts live performances happening in the space and publishes documentation and recordings afterwards.

Format

Each broadcast page combines a live stream link, a listen shelf of recordings, photo documentation from the night, spoken words or liner notes read during the performance, and a running audio library.

Infrastructure

Broadcasting infrastructure: AzuraCast (open source) · Hosted by FRP Radio.
