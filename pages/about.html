Radio Hectolitre

Radio Hectolitre is a web project constructed by a resident at Hectolitre, an artists-run space in Brussels. It broadcasts live performances happening in the space and publishes documentation and recordings afterwards.

Format

Each broadcast page combines a live stream link, a listen shelf of recordings, photo documentation from the night, spoken words or liner notes read during the performance, and a running audio library.
